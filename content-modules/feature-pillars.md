# FitCircle — Feature pillars

## Core engine
- AI-generated 30-day challenges, daily discussion prompts, newsletter drafts
- Member spotlight ideas to celebrate participants and grow social proof
- Cadence that keeps a fitness community from going quiet

## Workflow & output
- Community managers post without the daily blank-page friction
- Challenge + prompts + newsletter as a coherent engagement loop
- Review-and-post drafts instead of writing every piece by hand

## Trust & control
- Decision-support — you still approve what goes live
- No fabricated engagement or growth metrics
- Demo mode without a live API key
